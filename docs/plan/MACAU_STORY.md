## 3. 草稿文字（需使用者確認）

### 3.1 繁體中文

**一位真心的老師**

2010–2011 學年，Oakland 教授以訪問教授身分來到澳門大學，教我們臨床心理學碩士課程的認知評估。課堂之外，他也是我們的朋友。他真心看待每一位同學。他和我們討論未來，給了很多想法。他尊重每一個人。他相信可以建造一個更好的世界，也一直為此努力。

**什麼是專業**

他教給我最重要的一件事是：做事一定要專業。他讓我明白，一位心理學家要做到什麼程度，才算足夠專業。

**打開的世界**

因為是他的學生，我獲准到 University of South Florida 旁聽學校心理學博士班課程，並和心理學系的師生交流。這段經驗打開了我的眼界和世界觀。我立志成為一位專業的心理學家。

**最後的告別**

教授離世後，我和幾位他以前的學生一起到美國出席他的喪禮。我們也和他的家人一起整理他的遺物。今天，我的書架上仍放著從他書房帶回來的書和杯子。

**延續他的工作**

沒有他，我不會創立治療師公會，也不會成為臨床督導。他的離世也讓我開始研究犯罪心理學。現在，我為更生人士提供服務，也為警務人員提供犯罪心理學培訓。他對我的人生影響很大。

### 3.2 English

**A teacher who truly cared**

In the 2010–2011 academic year, Professor Oakland came to the University of Macau as a Visiting Professor and taught us Cognitive Assessment in the Master of Clinical Psychology program. Outside the classroom, he was also our friend. He cared about each of us. He talked with us about our futures and gave us many ideas. He respected everyone. He believed a better world was possible, and he worked for it.

**What it means to be professional**

The most important thing he taught me was that the work must always be done professionally. He showed me what it takes for a psychologist to be truly professional.

**A wider world**

Because I was his student, I was allowed to audit doctoral courses in school psychology at the University of South Florida and to meet faculty and students in the psychology department. That experience opened my eyes to the world. It made me decide to become a professional psychologist.

**A last goodbye**

After he died, I travelled to the United States with several of his former students to attend his funeral. We also helped his family sort through his belongings. Today, books and a mug from his study still sit on my bookshelf.

**Carrying on his work**

Without him, I would not have founded a therapists' association or become a clinical supervisor. His death also led me to research criminal psychology. Today I provide services for people rebuilding their lives after prison, and I train police officers in criminal psychology. He shaped my life profoundly.
