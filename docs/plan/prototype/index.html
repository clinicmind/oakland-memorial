Thomas D. Oakland

學校心理學家 · 測驗編製者 · 老師School psychologist · Test author · Teacher

他用一生研究怎樣公平地理解每一個孩子，也把這門專業帶到世界各地的教室。 He spent his life studying how to understand every child fairly, and he carried that work into classrooms around the world.
