---
Students, colleagues and friends can send a few lines about him: what he taught you, a moment you remember, how his work reached you.

## How it works

1. You write a few lines and choose a name, or stay anonymous.
2. You tell us how you knew him, for example student, colleague or friend.
3. You tick the box that allows us to publish your words.
4. We read every memory before it goes up. We do not publish anything without that tick.

Please do not name other people unless they have agreed. Please do not include details of his death.
