Every fact on this site has one or more of the sources below. Where the sources disagree, this page says so.

## Where sources differ

- **Retirement from the University of Florida.** The UF College of Education statement (S3) and Wikipedia (S1) say 2010. The family obituary (S2) and Fagan's tribute (S12) say 2013. This site states no year. It says only that he retired as Professor Emeritus.
- **Countries he worked in.** S8 says over forty. S3 says more than 45. S2 says more than 50, where he lived and worked. S12 says more than three dozen. This site says more than 40, the one wording all four support.
- **Number of tests, chapters and articles.** S2 counts 11 psychological tests; S8 counts five. S2 counts more than 200 chapters and articles; S3 counts 100 chapters and 200 articles; S8 counts more than 180. This site states none of these numbers.
- **When he joined the University of Texas at Austin.** S2 and S12 say 1967. No source we opened gives another year.
