## A last goodbye

After he died, I travelled to the United States with several of his former students to attend his funeral. We also helped his family sort through his belongings. Today, books and a mug from his study still sit on my bookshelf.

## Carrying on his work

Without him, I would not have founded a therapists' association or become a clinical supervisor. His death also led me to research criminal psychology. Today I provide services for people rebuilding their lives after prison, and I train police officers in criminal psychology. He shaped my life profoundly.
