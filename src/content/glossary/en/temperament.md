---
title: "Temperament"
description: "The early, largely inborn base of personality, such as energy level and mood."
factIds: [F30]
---
Temperament is the basic foundation of personality. It is usually seen as biologically based and present early in life [N-G-10]. In school settings, it helps explain why students differ in how they prefer to work and learn. Oakland co-authored the Student Styles Questionnaire, which looks at individual differences in students' temperaments and personal styles (F30).
