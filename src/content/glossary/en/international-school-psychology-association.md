---
title: "International School Psychology Association (ISPA)"
description: "A worldwide association that promotes school psychology and children's well-being."
factIds: [F10, F34]
---
ISPA was officially founded in 1982, and it can trace its roots to 1972 [N-G-19]. It is a non-governmental organization affiliated to UNESCO, and it works to spread school psychology across countries [N-G-19]. Oakland served as its president, from 1995 to 1997.
