---
title: "Cultural fairness and bias"
description: "Whether a test treats people from different cultures fairly, or favours the values of one group."
---
Cultural bias is the tendency to judge things by the values and beliefs of one's own community [N-G-9]. In testing, it can make a test easier for one cultural group than for another. Assessors look for such bias in content, language and norms, and they aim for cultural fairness.
