---
title: "Psychoeducational assessment"
description: "The gathering of data about a student's abilities and behavior to guide educational decisions."
---
Psychological assessment gathers and combines data to evaluate a person's behavior and abilities [N-G-14]. In schools, the same work is called psychoeducational assessment, and it includes giving psychoeducational tests [N-G-12]. Its results help teachers and families plan support for a student.
