---
title: "Conceptual, social and practical domains"
description: "The three groups of skills that make up adaptive behavior."
factIds: [F20]
---
The conceptual domain covers language, literacy, and money, time and number concepts. The social domain covers interpersonal skills, social responsibility and rule following. The practical domain covers daily living, work, health, travel and safety skills [N-G-2]. The ABAS-3 groups its adaptive skill areas into these three domains (F20).
