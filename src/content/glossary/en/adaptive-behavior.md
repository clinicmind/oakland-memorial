---
title: "Adaptive behavior"
description: "The everyday skills people use to meet the demands of daily life and fulfil social roles."
factIds: [F19]
---
Adaptive behavior is how well a person handles everyday tasks and meets the expectations of their community [N-G-1]. It covers conceptual, social and practical skills [N-G-2]. School psychologists rate it next to intellectual functioning when they consider a diagnosis of intellectual disability. The ABAS-3, co-authored by Oakland, is a rating system for adaptive skills.
