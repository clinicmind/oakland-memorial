---
title: "Norm-referenced assessment"
description: "An assessment in which a score is read by comparing it with a norm group."
---
In norm-referenced assessment, a score gains meaning when it is compared with a norm, usually the average of a specified group [N-G-6]. The result says how a person performed relative to others, not whether they met a fixed goal. This is why a norm group must fit the person being assessed.
